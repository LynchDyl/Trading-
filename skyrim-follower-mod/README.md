# Sunniva — Custom Follower (Skyrim SE/AE)

A small, self-contained Skyrim Special Edition mod that adds **Sunniva**, a
young blonde Nord follower inspired by a photo — wavy fair hair, a dark
blue dress (Tavern Clothes), and boots. She is essential (can't be killed),
levels with you, and can be recruited as a follower the normal way.

The whole mod is a single ESL-flagged plugin, `SunnivaFollower.esp`
(~650 bytes), generated by `generate_esp.py`. No Creation Kit, no loose
files, no load-order slot used.

## Install

**Vortex (Blood and Lust collection users):** drag `SunnivaFollower.esp`
into Vortex's drop zone (or *Install From File*), enable it, and deploy.
Load order position doesn't matter — it only adds new records.

**MO2:** zip the `.esp` and *Install a new mod from an archive*, or drop the
`.esp` into a new empty mod folder. Enable the plugin.

**Manual:** copy `SunnivaFollower.esp` into `Skyrim Special Edition/Data/`
and enable it in the mod menu.

Requires Skyrim SE 1.5.3 or newer (any AE version is fine).

## Meeting her

She isn't pre-placed in the world, so summon her once with the console
(`~` key):

```
help Sunniva 4
```

Note the ID shown next to the `NPC_` result (it starts with `FE` because the
plugin is ESL-flagged — e.g. `FE089800`), then:

```
player.placeatme FE089800
```

She appears next to you; from then on she's a normal persistent NPC. Talk to
her and choose **"Follow me. I need your help."** to recruit her, like any
vanilla follower.

## Design notes (why she looks the way she does)

A photo-accurate face requires sculpting in RaceMenu/Creation Kit and
exporting FaceGen meshes — an ESP built outside the CK that defines its own
face gets the infamous **grey face bug**. To keep this plugin bulletproof,
Sunniva's appearance, voice, and stats are **templated from Jordis the
Sword-Maiden** (Skyrim.esm `0x000A2C8F`) — the closest vanilla match: a
young, blonde, fair-skinned Nord woman. Templating reuses Bethesda's shipped
FaceGen, so her face always renders correctly.

Two nice side effects for a heavily modded setup like **Blood and Lust**:

- Any NPC appearance overhaul in your list that retouches Jordis
  automatically applies to Sunniva too, so she gets the modlist's
  high-res beautified look rather than the 2011 vanilla face.
- She uses the default Nord body and skin, so your body/texture replacers
  apply to her out of the box.

Everything else is her own: name, essential/unique flags, follower factions
(`PotentialFollowerFaction` / `CurrentFollowerFaction` at rank −1), an
ally relationship with the player (that's what unlocks the recruit dialogue),
and a custom outfit record — Tavern Clothes (`0x000D191F`, the dark blue
dress) and Fine Boots (`0x00086993`), echoing the denim dress and white
cowboy boots in the photo. Follower frameworks (e.g. Nether's Follower
Framework, if your list includes one) pick her up like any standard
recruitable NPC.

## Customizing

Regenerate the plugin after editing `generate_esp.py`:

```
python3 generate_esp.py   # rewrites SunnivaFollower.esp
python3 validate_esp.py   # structural sanity check
```

Easy tweaks near the top of the script:

- **Name** — change `"Sunniva"` in the `FULL` subrecord (and `SNAM`
  description if you like).
- **Outfit** — swap the two FormIDs in the `OTFT` record for any vanilla
  clothing/armor.
- **Face** — to give her a truly custom face later, open the plugin in the
  Creation Kit, clear the traits template flag, sculpt, and press
  `Ctrl+F4` to export FaceGen. Or change `JORDIS` to another female Nord's
  base ID to borrow a different vanilla face.

You can also open `SunnivaFollower.esp` in xEdit (SSEEdit) — it loads clean —
and edit any of this directly.

## Files

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `SunnivaFollower.esp` | The mod — this is the only file the game needs |
| `generate_esp.py` | Builds the `.esp` from scratch (stdlib Python only) |
| `validate_esp.py` | Parses the `.esp` back and verifies its structure |
